`<Approvals>` shows what approving a policy-set proposal does. When the open approval is `tool:noblecloak:propose-policy-set`, the detail gets a panel above the full call and the Approve and Deny buttons: the proposed policies grouped by subject (Everyone, each role, agents) with the tool, the mode and the row kind; what changes, from the `preview` the single read serves to a reader who may approve (each loosening as "subject on tool: from → to", or "Approving loosens no policy."); the role rows that have no effect; the tools granted, the tools to register and the agent to bind. A reader who is served the call but no preview sees the call's parts and no claim about loosening. A call or preview that is present but not the contract's shape is not summarised: the full call is shown with the module's "could not read" message. Every other approval is unchanged. The words are new `labels` keys (`proposal…`), the panel renders through the `Card` slot with the class `nc-proposal`, and the stylesheet gains rules for it. `openDetail(id)`, which already opens one approval's detail by id whether or not the queue lists it, is now documented as the deep-link call.
